Privacy Policy

Pip the Pet · Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version

Pip the Pet does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your pet's name, animal, colours, stats and history are saved only on your iPhone, so the app and its Home Screen widget can share them. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Pip schedules reminders on your iPhone (for example when your pet is about to get hungry). They're created on your device and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

Pip the Pet is suitable for all ages and does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions? Send us a message.
