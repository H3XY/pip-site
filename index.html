Pip the Pet

A little handheld pet for your iPhone

Looking after Pip

  • Feed a meal to fill hunger hearts, or a snack for happiness (but snacks add weight).
  • Play Higher or Lower: win 3 of 5 rounds for a happiness heart.
  • Clean up messes, and give medicine if Pip gets sick (two doses).
  • When Pip falls asleep, tap the light to turn it off.
  • Sometimes Pip calls for attention without needing anything. Scold then to build training.
  • Tap Pip to give a pat. The bell blinks when Pip needs you.

Make it yours

In Settings, choose your animal (Bear, Bunny, Kitty, Chick, Frog or Penguin), the colour of the handheld, and the screen colour. Add the Pip widget to your Home Screen to care for Pip without opening the app. On iPhone Duo, open your phone to see Pip on one screen and the controls on the other.
